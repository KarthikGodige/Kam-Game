1. Open the folder `C:\Users\hi\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\bro-revenge-game` in VS Code.
2. If you have the **"Live Server"** extension installed in VS Code:

## 🎭 How to Roast Your Friend

1. Click **"🎭 Customize"** on the menu or HUD.
2. Enter your friend's name (e.g. *Rahul*, *Alex*, *Chomu*).
3. Upload his funniest/most embarrassing photo or click **"📷 Camera Snap"** to take a picture of him.
4. Pick a prop like the **🤡 Clown Wig** or **💩 Poop Hat**.
5. Hit **"SAVE & START ROASTING"**.
6. When he crashes, click **"💬 Send Roast to Friend"** to send a savage pre-formatted roast straight to his WhatsApp!
7. Click **"📸 Download Roast Card"** to save a generated picture certificate with his face, clown stamp, and score!
